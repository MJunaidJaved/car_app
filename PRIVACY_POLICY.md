# ShareWay Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

ShareWay is a ride-sharing app for passengers and captains. The app uses account, location, ride, contact, notification, and profile information only to provide ride posting, booking, fare negotiation, live ride tracking, safety contact, and app notification features.

## Data We Collect

- Account data: name, email, phone number, role, gender, city, and profile details.
- Captain verification data: CNIC/identity images, vehicle information, registration details, and verification status.
- Location data: pickup/drop coordinates, nearby ride search location, and live ride tracking location during active rides.
- Ride data: posted rides, customer ride requests, fare offers, bookings, ride status, ratings, wallet activity, and notifications.
- Media/uploads: profile, vehicle, or document images uploaded by the user.

## How We Use Data

- To create and manage passenger and captain accounts.
- To show nearby rides and customer ride requests.
- To let passengers and captains negotiate fares and confirm rides.
- To show phone/WhatsApp contact after a booking or customer request is accepted.
- To track active rides and improve safety during trips.
- To send ride, offer, booking, and account notifications.
- To support fraud prevention, verification, and customer support.

## Location Use

ShareWay requests location permission to show nearby rides, nearby customer ride requests, map pickup/drop selection, and active ride tracking. Location is used for app functionality and is not sold.

## Data Sharing

ShareWay does not sell personal data. Limited ride/contact details may be shared between a passenger and captain only when needed for booking, fare negotiation, accepted rides, WhatsApp/call contact, and live ride tracking.

## Third-Party Services

ShareWay may use Firebase, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps/Places, Cloudinary/Firebase Storage, and image upload services to operate the app. These services process data according to their own privacy and security policies.

## Data Retention

Expired rides and customer requests may be automatically removed or marked completed/cancelled according to ride status. Account, ride, booking, wallet, and verification records may be retained as needed for support, safety, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

## User Controls

Users can update profile data in the app. Customers can delete open ride requests. Captains can cancel active posted rides where allowed. Users may contact support to request help with account or data issues.
